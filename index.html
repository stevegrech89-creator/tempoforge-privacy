Privacy policy

This policy explains how TempoForge handles your training information, optional Pro access, backups and support requests.

Effective date: 27 September 2026

App: TempoForge · com.tempoforge.app

Publisher: Digital Apps SG

Support and privacy: digitalapps.sg@gmail.com

Training information on your device

TempoForge stores the information you create or enter: workout programs, exercises, activity names, reusable blocks, interval timers, schedules, completed sessions, sets, repetitions, weights, timing records, optional distance, notes and app preferences. It also stores active-session checkpoints to recover an unfinished workout. This information is used to build, plan, run and review your training.

Training data is kept in private app storage on your Android device. There is no TempoForge account or publisher-operated cloud training service, and the app does not send your training records to Digital Apps SG.

TempoForge adds no advertising, publisher-operated analytics or remote crash-reporting integration. Google Play billing uses supporting SDKs and operational data flows described below. TempoForge does not access Health Connect, import other health data, request location or sensor permissions, record GPS routes, or read heart-rate measurements. Distance is entered manually.

Timing and notifications

TempoForge uses Android notifications, a foreground service and a temporary wake lock to support a workout you start, including timer and rest cues when the screen is off. Notifications can show session information according to your device’s notification settings. These permissions do not give TempoForge access to health records or location.

Google Play billing and network access

TempoForge Pro is an optional one-time lifetime Google Play purchase in the same app. Google Play processes purchases using your Google account and its own payment practices. TempoForge does not receive your payment-card details or Google account password through the app.

The billing integration sends product queries, purchase and restore requests, and purchase acknowledgements to Google Play. It receives information such as the product ID, purchase token, purchase status, purchase time and signed receipt. The app verifies that information and keeps the signed receipt in separate private app storage for offline access and restoration. Receipts are excluded from training backups. Pending payments do not unlock Pro.

Billing requests do not include your workout programs, exercise logs, training history, notes or manual distance. TempoForge does not operate a publisher billing server.

The Google Play Billing SDK and supporting Google SDKs add internet and network-status access to the app. They may communicate with Google for billing operation and reliability. Google may process purchase activity and app, device, connection and diagnostic information under its own practices. The Google Privacy Policy describes Google’s processing and available controls.

Complimentary Pro access codes

If you have a complimentary Pro access code, TempoForge verifies it on your device and stores the signed code in private app storage, separately from training backups and Google Play receipts. Verification does not send the code or your training records to a server.

The code grants access to the same Pro features without changing Google Play purchase ownership. You can remove it from the Pro card. Clearing app storage or uninstalling also removes it. Keep your code if you want to activate it again.

Support messages

You can contact digitalapps.sg@gmail.com. Information you choose to send is used to respond to your request and is processed through the mailbox provider. Training backups are not sent automatically.

We retain support messages only as needed to handle your request and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion by emailing the same address. Exported backups and information held by Google or your selected storage provider are handled separately.

Backup files you choose to create

Export creates a readable JSON file containing your saved training data, history and preferences in a destination you select with Android’s document picker. The file is not password protected or encrypted by TempoForge. Active-session checkpoints, purchase receipts and entitlements, and complimentary access codes are excluded.

If you select a cloud document provider or later share the file, that provider or recipient can receive its contents under their own terms and privacy practices. TempoForge does not automatically upload a copy to its publisher. Keep exported files somewhere you trust and share them only when you intend to share the training information inside.

Restore reads a backup file you select. After validation and your confirmation, its saved data replaces the current training library, plans, history and preferences. TempoForge disables Android automatic cloud backup and device-transfer backup for its private app data.

Retention and deletion

Your local data remains until you remove it, restore a backup that replaces it, clear TempoForge’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app. You can delete saved programs and sessions in the app. Deleting a saved program preserves completed session history; delete those sessions separately if you want to remove them from history.

Android’s Clear storage action or uninstalling TempoForge removes its private local data, including recovery information, complimentary access codes and cached purchase receipts. Clearing local data does not cancel a Google Play purchase or erase Google’s transaction records. A valid purchase can be restored through Google Play.

These actions do not delete JSON files you exported or copies held by other people or storage providers. Delete those copies separately. Digital Apps SG cannot retrieve or erase your device’s offline training records remotely. There is no TempoForge user account to delete.

This privacy-policy website

This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs and stores visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, including when visitors are not signed in to GitHub. See GitHub Pages’ data collection information and the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Digital Apps SG has not added analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies or embedded third-party media to this page. Visiting this page does not give GitHub access to the training records stored inside your TempoForge app.

Protection and policy changes

TempoForge uses Android’s private app storage and requests only permissions used for its current features. The safety of the device and of any exported backup also depends on the device lock and storage destination you choose. TempoForge does not add its own encryption layer to local records or exported files.

If TempoForge’s data practices change, the updated policy will identify its effective date. This policy and the in-app privacy information will describe the released behavior, including any future accounts, online features, analytics or integrations before they are introduced.
